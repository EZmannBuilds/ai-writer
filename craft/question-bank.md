# Storytelling Question Bank
## AI Writer v0.4

The agent uses these mostly **internally**.

Do not dump the questionnaire on the user.

## Story questions

- Why this story?
- Why now?
- What becomes unstable?
- What does the protagonist want that produces action?
- What will they sacrifice for it?
- What do they misunderstand?
- What contradiction makes them interesting?
- What is the reader promised?
- What question should remain open longest?

## Character questions

- What do they want today?
- What do they want from this specific person?
- What will they never admit?
- What do they believe that is wrong?
- What information are they missing?
- What are they ashamed of?
- What would they notice first in this room?
- What option do they refuse to consider?
- What would make that option necessary?

## Scene questions

- Who wants what?
- Why now?
- What resists?
- What is the hidden pressure?
- What is the hidden sentence?
- What changes?
- What new problem exists afterward?
- Can I start later?
- Can I end earlier?
- What should be shown?
- What should be told?
- What one detail anchors the scene?
- What is the current psychic distance?

## Dialogue questions

- What are they literally talking about?
- What are they really negotiating?
- What is unsafe to say?
- What tactic is each using?
- Who has more status?
- Who needs the other more?
- Which question gets dodged?
- Is this line for the other character, or only for the reader?
- Would silence be stronger?
- Do their voices differ without caricature?

## World / exposition questions

- What does the reader need right now?
- What can wait?
- What can be demonstrated before explained?
- What law/custom/technology/magic is currently doing something?
- Is lore interrupting the scene?
- What does the POV take for granted?

## Suspense / curiosity questions

- What does the reader want to know?
- What do they fear may happen?
- What does the character think will happen?
- Which option is closing?
- What evidence points two ways?
- When will this question be answered?
- What stronger question replaces it?

## Revision questions

- What paragraph is explaining what the scene already showed?
- What detail is generic?
- What detail is inventory?
- Where does POV drift?
- Which scene starts too early?
- Which scene ends too late?
- Which setup never pays?
- Which payoff needs a seed?
- Which character acts because the plot needs it?
- Which ending sentence explains the theme?
