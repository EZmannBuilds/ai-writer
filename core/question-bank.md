Internal diagnostics. The agent asks itself these; it asks the user only the few that are real creative forks.

## The question bank

These questions are primarily **internal agent diagnostics**.

Do not interrogate the user with all of them.

Ask the user only when:
- the answer is a true creative constraint;
- multiple choices would produce materially different requested works;
- context cannot reasonably resolve it.

Otherwise infer, draft, and revise.

### Premise

- Why this story?
- Why now?
- What becomes unstable?
- What is the reader promised?
- What is the central contradiction?
- What should remain unresolved?

### Character

- What does the character want today?
- Why do they want it?
- What do they think they need?
- What do they actually fear?
- What will they not admit?
- What do they misunderstand?
- What option would they never choose at the beginning?
- What could make that option imaginable later?

### Scene

- Who wants what?
- Why now?
- What resists?
- What tactic do they begin with?
- What makes them change tactic?
- What is the hidden sentence?
- What changes?
- What carries into the next scene?
- What can be cut from the beginning?
- What can be cut after the turn?
- What should be shown?
- What should be summarized?
- What is the one concrete anchor detail?
- What does the POV character notice that another would not?

### Dialogue

- What is the surface topic?
- What is the real topic?
- What can neither person safely say?
- Who has higher status?
- Who needs whom more?
- Is anyone answering a question nobody asked?
- Is exposition being spoken only for the reader?
- Would silence be stronger than this line?
- Do the voices actually differ?

### Backstory / World

- What does the reader need now?
- What can wait?
- What can be demonstrated before explained?
- What fact creates a question?
- What fact answers a question too soon?
- Is lore changing current action?

### Tension

- What might happen?
- What does the character think might happen?
- What does the reader think might happen?
- What is the cost of waiting?
- What is the cost of acting?
- What closes off an option?

### Description

- Why this detail?
- Who notices it?
- What does it imply?
- Is it specific or generic?
- Is it one detail too many?

### Ending

- What must close?
- What should stay open?
- What consequence has not yet landed?
- Is the theme being explained instead of left in the story?
- Did the character earn the change?

### Additional questions

Questions from the v0.4 quick card that the bank above did not already ask.

#### Story questions

- What does the protagonist want that produces action?
- What will they sacrifice for it?
- What contradiction makes them interesting?
- What question should remain open longest?

#### Character questions

- What do they want today?
- What do they want from this specific person?
- What will they never admit?
- What do they believe that is wrong?
- What information are they missing?
- What are they ashamed of?
- What would they notice first in this room?
- What option do they refuse to consider?
- What would make that option necessary?

#### Scene questions

- What is the hidden pressure?
- What new problem exists afterward?
- Can I start later?
- Can I end earlier?
- What should be told?
- What one detail anchors the scene?
- What is the current psychic distance?

#### Dialogue questions

- What are they literally talking about?
- What are they really negotiating?
- What is unsafe to say?
- What tactic is each using?
- Who has more status?
- Who needs the other more?
- Which question gets dodged?
- Is this line for the other character, or only for the reader?
- Would silence be stronger?
- Do their voices differ without caricature?

#### World / exposition questions

- What does the reader need right now?
- What law/custom/technology/magic is currently doing something?
- Is lore interrupting the scene?
- What does the POV take for granted?

#### Suspense / curiosity questions

- What does the reader want to know?
- What do they fear may happen?
- What does the character think will happen?
- Which option is closing?
- What evidence points two ways?
- When will this question be answered?
- What stronger question replaces it?

#### Revision questions

- What paragraph is explaining what the scene already showed?
- What detail is generic?
- What detail is inventory?
- Where does POV drift?
- Which scene starts too early?
- Which scene ends too late?
- Which setup never pays?
- Which payoff needs a seed?
- Which character acts because the plot needs it?
- Which ending sentence explains the theme?
