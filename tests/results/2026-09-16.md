# Behavioral run — 2026-09-16

- **Skill:** v0.7. Round 1 ran at commit `2140879`; later rounds ran on the working tree with the fixes listed below, committed together with this file.
- **Model:** Claude Opus 5 for both executor and grader agents; each role ran in a separate context.
- **Protocol:** [tests/README.md](../README.md#running-the-behavioral-suite). Executors saw only the request and situation. Graders saw the fixture and the response.

## Result

**35 / 35 fixtures pass in their final grading.** That total is not one clean run; it took three rounds, and seven round-1 failures led to real changes.

| Round | Scope | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | F01–F34, all executed and graded | 27 / 34 |
| 2 | F08, F16, F19, F20, F21, F23 re-executed after skill fixes; F06, F11, F18, F32 regraded after fixture fixes; F32 and new F35 executed after the unsupplied-canon fix | 8 / 10 (F20 and F32 fail) |
| 3 | F20, F32 re-executed after further skill fixes | 2 / 2 |

**Caveat.** The 25 fixtures that passed in round 1 were **not re-run** after the round-2 and round-3 skill changes. Those changes were additive:

- plans stay hidden in FAST SCENE MODE;
- the Tragic engine must give three non-death options;
- the research overlay is not a task;
- the Draft Starter gate wording changed;
- referenced-but-unsupplied canon is not invented;
- research items get a kind plus a status;
- suspect records must be complete.

None of these should change those 25 outcomes, but this run doesn't prove it.

## Grader concerns not captured by criteria

- **F16 fairness gap:** the round-2 plan's solution relies on one suspect's absence that only his own denial supports, and one entry method isn't explained. The fairness tests were listed but not applied rigorously. A model-execution weakness, not a missing rule.
- **F32 clue gap:** round 3's false solution needs investigators to learn something that only the victim knew.
- **F21:** loaded Story Genome and candidate search for a picture-book story; nothing showed in the output, but it's heavier than needed.
- **F23:** the horror task appears only in a comment in the route block.
- **Name reuse:** "Moss" and "Ines" recur across independent runs — possible default names.
- **F20:** "don't research" items were left unlabeled; the grader read them as research decisions rather than story details.
