## Entries

### `friedman-show-tell`

- **Source:** Jane Friedman (site), "What Do We Really Mean When We Say 'Show, Don't Tell'?". https://janefriedman.com/what-do-we-really-mean-when-we-say-show-dont-tell/
- **Type:** craft-practice
- **Status:** recorded in v0.4; not re-verified in v0.6
- **Claim:** Telling remains useful; showing matters most in high-value scene moments; immersion depends on clear action and emotion.
- **Derives:** The show–tell decision engine: dramatize what the reader should experience, compress what they only need to know.
- **Limitations:** Craft commentary; not empirical.
- **Fidelity:** convention — a craft practice applied as a diagnostic.
