# Research: Tragic Anime Story Engine

Pack: [packs/tragic-anime-engine.md](../../packs/tragic-anime-engine.md) and its [lenses](../../packs/tragic-anime-lenses.md).

The pack extracts **high-level craft patterns** from official publisher and streaming descriptions and from creator/editor interviews. It never recreates a work's plot, characters, terminology, or voice. Descriptions of what a work *does* are the pack authors' readings of those sources, not claims the sources make about craft.

**Design conclusion shared across every lens:** create something worth preserving, put it under coherent pressure, make characters choose, enforce the cost, and let the consequences remain in the story.

## Primary pillars

### `kodansha-aot`

- **Source:** Kodansha, *Attack on Titan* series page and Omnibus Vol. 12 page. https://kodansha.us/series/attack-on-titan/ ; https://kodansha.us/series/attack-on-titan-omnibus/volume-12/
- **Type:** official-description
- **Status:** recorded in the pack's v1.0 research notes (2026-09-15); not re-verified in v0.6
- **Claim:** Official material frames the early story as survival against an apparently external monstrous threat, and later frames the ending around a cycle of fear, oppression, and destruction.
- **Derives:** Escalating truth; recontextualization; inherited cycles; a conflict that changes from monster survival to human and political conflict.
- **Limitations:** Publisher marketing copy; the craft reading is the pack's.
- **Fidelity:** conceptual

### `isayama-interview-2023`

- **Source:** Crunchyroll News, interview with Hajime Isayama, 2023-01-13. https://www.crunchyroll.com/news/interviews/2023/1/13/interview-attack-on-titan-creator-hajime-isayama-reflects-on-the-series-ending-game-of-thrones-and-more
- **Type:** creator-interview
- **Status:** recorded in the pack's v1.0 research notes (2026-09-15); not re-verified in v0.6
- **Claim:** Isayama describes deliberately shifting to the side first presented as the enemy, letting the audience come to understand it, then reintroducing the original protagonists as enemies from that viewpoint.
- **Derives:** The six-stage perspective-reversal module.
- **Limitations:** One interview; a creator's account of intent.
- **Fidelity:** conceptual
