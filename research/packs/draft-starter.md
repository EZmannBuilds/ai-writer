# Research: Draft Starter

Pack: [packs/draft-starter.md](../../packs/draft-starter.md).

Draft Starter shipped without external sources, and none were added. Its rules are **interaction-design decisions** for collaborative writing: ask few, consequential questions; let the user delegate; keep provenance visible. They are recorded here as design so no one mistakes them for findings.

## Entries

### `design-draft-starter`

- **Source:** AI Writer design decision (Draft Starter v1.0, 2026-09-15).
- **Type:** design
- **Status:** n/a
- **Claim:** A short, emotional-core-first interview (3–5 questions, at most two rounds) converts a loose seed into a usable brief better than a questionnaire or an immediate draft.
- **Derives:** The question layers, round limits, creative-control modes, the Draft Brief, and the provenance lists.
- **Limitations:** Untested against alternatives. The regression fixtures check that the behavior happens, not that it produces better stories; the [benchmark](../benchmark.md) is where that would be measured.
- **Fidelity:** design — no external source.
