A flexible writing workflow, **not a publisher-submission guarantee**. Artists, editors, publishers, and webtoon platforms may require different formats.

## Entries

### `darkhorse-script-guide`

- **Source:** Dark Horse Comics, "Script Format and Specifications" (PDF). https://images.darkhorse.com/darkhorse08/company/submissions/scriptguide.pdf
- **Type:** publisher-guide
- **Status:** recorded in the pack's v1.0 research notes (2026-09-15); not re-verified in v0.6
- **Claim:** Clearly labeled pages, numbered panels, concise descriptions, dialogue labels, reading-order treatment for dialogue, SFX, and captions, and rough dialogue-density guidance.
- **Derives:** The full-script house format; word counts as soft warnings.
- **Limitations:** One publisher's guide.
- **Fidelity:** convention

### `woodman-maynard-page-turns`

- **Source:** K. Woodman-Maynard, "Page Turns: Crafting Suspense in Comics," 2024-12-18. https://www.woodmanmaynard.com/blog/2024/12/18/page-turns-crafting-suspense-in-comics
- **Type:** craft-practice
- **Status:** recorded in the pack's v1.0 research notes (2026-09-15); not re-verified in v0.6
- **Claim:** A reveal loses impact if it is visible on the current spread before its setup is read, so creators plan spreads.
- **Derives:** Page-turn engine; spread awareness; no assumed odd/even parity.
- **Limitations:** One cartoonist's discussion.
- **Fidelity:** convention
